- **Short Description** (one or two sentences)
- **Problem** it solves
- **Target Users**
- **Tech Stack**
- **Current Status** (idea / prototype / MVP / production)
- **Completed Work**
- **Current Challenges**
- **Next Steps**
- **Important Decisions** (with a short reason each)
- **Notes for AI**

Only use what I tell you. If a field is unknown, leave a clear placeholder instead of guessing. Keep it short and practical, then add it to `projects.md`.
